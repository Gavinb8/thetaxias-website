Building the Theta Xi legacy

Since our founding on October 3, 1948, the Alpha Sigma Chapter has been a staple of Bradley University’s Greek community and a home away from home for generations of brothers. At the heart of the brotherhood are the members of our collegiate chapter, who keep Alpha Sigma’s standing on campus strong.

Today, our brothers face rising costs for tuition, textbooks and everything else college asks of them. Alumni and undergraduate leadership built a long-term plan to answer that: merit-based scholarships and a competitive place to live.

A dedicated scholarship fund through the Theta Xi Foundation has been growing since 2006. Since 2011 we have awarded nearly $200,000 to qualified brothers, and we give more scholarship money to Bradley students than any other fraternity on campus.
