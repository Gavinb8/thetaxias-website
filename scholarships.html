Scholarships that pay real tuition.

Alpha Sigma holds the Theta Xi Foundation’s largest scholarship fund of any chapter nationwide. Last year we gave brothers $52,700 in scholarships, more than any other fraternity at Bradley.

A PORTAL to success

The goal of our scholarships is to create a PORTAL to success for every brother.

Pay-it-forward

Our scholarships only exist through the generosity of those who have gone before. They grow when the brothers who benefited choose to pay it forward and join our donors after graduation.

Occupancy

Theta Xi’s purpose is to provide a college home for its members. A competitive and safe college home needs occupants.

Recruitment

Recruitment is the lifeblood of every Greek organization. We can’t keep going on campus without new members.

Thrive, not just survive

College is so expensive that many students barely get through it working long hours. Our scholarships let brothers cut back on work and take a full part in academics, leadership and activities.

Academics

The first purpose of Theta Xi is intellectual curiosity and the highest scholarship rating each brother is capable of. All of our scholarships are merit-based with GPA minimums.

Leadership

Brothers build real leadership skills by practicing democratic self-government in the chapter.

Membership scholarships

Our chapter and alumni are committed to helping hard-working brothers pay for school. The Janus and Gleespen scholarships are endowments that grow through investment and donations.
