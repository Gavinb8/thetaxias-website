Scholarships that pay real tuition.

Alpha Sigma holds the Theta Xi Foundation’s largest scholarship fund of any chapter nationwide. Last year we gave brothers $49,975 in scholarships, more than any other fraternity at Bradley.

Membership scholarships

Our chapter and alumni are committed to helping hard-working brothers pay for school. The Janus and Gleespen scholarships are endowments that grow through investment and donations.

$4,000

each year, to five brothers

Janus Scholarship

For members living in the chapter house with a GPA above 3.0.

  • Lives in house
  • GPA 3.0+

$1,200

per semester, renewable for up to 8 semesters

Blue Iris Scholarship

A four-year scholarship issued each fall to two or three newly initiated brothers with a cumulative GPA above 3.25.

  • New members
  • GPA 3.25+

$500

each year, to three brothers

Gary Gleespen Scholarship

For brothers showing a high level of academic performance or improvement.

  • Academic achievement

$50 / $100

every semester, to every qualifying member

Senator Society Scholarship

$50 to each member with a semester GPA of 3.5 or higher, and $100 to each member with a 4.0.

  • GPA 3.5+
  • 4.0 bonus

Book Scholarship: open to all of Bradley

Two $500 scholarships each fall for any incoming freshman or sophomore. Recipients are chosen on involvement in extracurricular activities and leadership roles. You don’t need to be a member.
