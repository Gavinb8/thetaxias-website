Scholarships

The Alpha Sigma Chapter is the Theta Xi Foundation’s largest scholarship fund of all Theta Xi Chapters nationwide. Our chapter and alumni are committed to assisting hard-working students pay for school. Last academic year alone, we distributed $50,000 in scholarships, which is more money per member than any other fraternity on Bradley's campus. As years go on, we expect to increase our scholarship pool as well.

The Janus and Gleespen scholarships are funded through endowments that grow via investments and donations. Tax-deductible donations to these endowments can be made through the Theta Xi Foundation.

Current Scholarships

Book Scholarship

An endowment awarding two $500 scholarships to any incoming Freshman (2027) or Sophomore (2026). Affiliation with Theta Xi is not required, and recipients will be determined by extracurricular activities and leadership roles held. Scholarships awarded early in the Fall Semester

Janus Scholarship

An endowment that awards $5,000 annually to three brothers each living in the chapter house with a GPA greater than 3.0.

Blue Iris Scholarship

A four-year scholarship issued each fall. This fall it will award $1,200 / semester scholarships to three newly initiated brothers with GPAs greater than 3.25.

Gary Gleespen Scholarship

An endowment that awards more than $500 each year to multiple brothers showing a high level of academic performance or improvement.
