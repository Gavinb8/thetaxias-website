Joining The Fraternity

Since 1948, the men of Theta Xi at Bradley University have always been actively seeking out the best and brightest men on campus to continue the proud traditions of the Fraternity. Other than a minimum GPA of 2.5 at Bradley, the requirements for membership are a strong sense of academic, moral and self-responsibility.

Membership in Theta Xi means an experience like no other in your college life. We strive to make the most of our stays at Bradley through our excellence in academics, social and service activities outside the classroom, leadership opportunities, and brothers who will be friends for life.

Students at Bradley interested in membership are invited to meet our members and decide if Theta Xi is right for them. Contact our recruitment chairman at rc.thetaxi@gmail.com or fill out our membership inquiry form below if you’d like to visit the house or join in one of our many social activities.

Alumni, we value your referrals. If you know of good men on campus who have not joined a fraternity or of high school seniors headed to Bradley University, please recommend and impress upon them the values of Theta Xi, Alpha Sigma. You may submit prospect recommendations using the form below.
