Since 1948, the men of Theta Xi have looked for the best and brightest on campus to carry on the chapter’s traditions. If that sounds like you, we’d like to meet you.

What we look for

Beyond a minimum 2.5 GPA at Bradley, membership asks for a strong sense of responsibility in three areas.

Academic

Brothers take school seriously, and the scholarship program rewards it.

Moral

We hold each other to a standard and expect members to own their choices.

Personal

Leadership roles, service and a full social calendar, with brothers who stay friends for life.

How to get to know us

No commitment to start. Come meet people and decide for yourself.

  1. Reach out

    Email our recruitment chairman or send the form below.

  2. Visit the house

    Stop by 1317 W. Fredonia Ave. and see where brothers live.

  3. Join an event

    Come to one of our social or service events and meet the chapter.
